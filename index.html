About / 关于Beijing, China

From individual traits to multidimensional adaptation.

My research focuses on plant functional traits and their coordination. I use trait networks and large-scale data synthesis to investigate how plants adapt across biodiversity, climate and habitat gradients.

我的研究聚焦植物功能性状及其相互联系，通过性状网络与多维性状整合，探索植物生态适应及其在群落尺度上的变化。
